3A · Motion worker

Compare two images

As soon as ready

Use the previous processed frame and the newest small frame. No fixed rate or intentional pause.

Choose a frame

  • Wait for a new sequence number.
  • First image: warm-up; no shift yet.
  • Pair gap ≤500 ms: attempt analysis. Larger gap: reset the reference.

Accept a shift

  • Patch intensity stddev ≥3; peak quality ≥8.
  • |dx| ≤20 px and |dy| ≤14 px per 80×56 patch.
  • At least four valid patches, then four within 1.5 px of their median displacement.

A weak estimate becomes “unreliable”; its image can still be displayed. Wrong array shape or nonfinite image data raises an analysis error.

3B · Detector worker

Recognize objects

Starts ≥500 ms apart

At most about 2 starts/second. Choose the newest full frame at each start. Slow inference lowers the rate.

Choose a frame

  • Wait until the next start is due.
  • Require a new source sequence.
  • Skip a source older than 1.5 seconds.
  • Intermediate frames are bypassed intentionally; no detection backlog.

Accept boxes

  • Valid model row and class; background ignored.
  • Score ≥0.5; positive box area after clipping to the image.

Zero accepted boxes is a valid detection result. Malformed model output raises a detector error.

3C · IMU worker

Read angular rate

Read, then pause 50 ms

A 2.4 ms read plus the pause gives about 19.1 samples/second. Host timestamps use each read's midpoint.

Accept a sample

  • Three finite gyro values.
  • Finite, strictly increasing timestamp.

Startup offset

  • Keep the rig still for the initial ~1-second window.
  • At least 10 samples; per-axis stddev ≤0.5°/s.
  • Excessive variation leaves an error requiring a restart.

After calibration, intentional rotation is valid data. This startup stillness check does not reject ordinary later movement.

Gyro samples have S-numbers here for teaching; no camera F-number

4 · Package the latest available results

State.snapshot()

The browser requests GET /api/frame. The web server's handler calls State.snapshot(), which gathers the latest motion result plus detector.snapshot() and imu.snapshot().

Motion freshness

Source age >2 s: stale, hide dx/dy. No analyzed frame after 10 s: startup error.

Box freshness

Source age >1.5 s, detector error, or camera unavailable: hide boxes. No initial detector result after 10 s: stale.

IMU freshness

Sample age >300 ms: stale, hide gyro. No samples after 2 s: error. Calibration must finish before corrected rates are shown.

Locks protect consistent updates to each result. Combining these results does not synchronize their original observations. The motion image and boxes retain their own source sequences.
